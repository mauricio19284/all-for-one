                                        Daily quests

                                          Every stat owns a yearly plan and sends one quest a day — tap a quest to open its instructions. Gym and the must-finish tick themselves; the rest come from the plans by program day, week and month, so easier weeks, test days, benchmarks and monthly reviews land on the right date. Quests you add here stay every day. 60 XP moves a stat one point; a stat ignored for more than two days starts slipping. A missed day carries nothing over — the plans say so.
