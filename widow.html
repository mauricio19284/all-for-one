    Widow's call

      Pick a stock and send Widow. It reads the chart, the numbers and the news, then says buy, avoid or pass, and why. Five trading days later the call is graded.

      What Widow has learned

      The track record counts every graded call by the evidence behind it. The built-in rules lean on a setup more or less once it has six graded calls, and Claude gets the record and the lessons before each new call.

      Scoreboard

      Prices are generated. Real news here leads the price about two times in three by design, and some headlines are traps. A good score here does not prove an edge with real money.
